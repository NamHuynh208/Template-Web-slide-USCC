## Layout guide

- `lead`: one short paragraph below the title.
- `columns`: up to three equal points; keep each column short.
- `split`: two balanced regions, often text plus one local figure.
- `statement`: one concise takeaway, not a paragraph.
- `list`: labeled points with short descriptions.
- `timeline`: up to four phases with title, time, and one-line description.

If a section contains more material than fits the safe area, create another slide.
Do not solve overflow by reducing the template font sizes.
